# LinkedIn

Most CTF platforms test whether you can exploit a vulnerability.
Almost none teach you *why it exists* before you try.

That's the gap I just closed in LLMVault v1.1.0.

LLMVault is a hands-on training range for the OWASP Top 10 for LLM
Applications — 25 labs across three tiers, built the way WebGoat taught
web security: learn the fix by practicing the break.

What's new:

→ Every lab now opens with a short reference panel for its OWASP
category: what the issue is, why it happens, and what to watch for in
real-world systems — before you touch the chat window.
→ Flag verification now checks a hash of your submission instead of
comparing plaintext.
→ A visual refresh across the dashboard.

It's a small release. But it changes the order people learn in: concept
first, exploit second. That's the difference between memorizing a
payload and actually understanding prompt injection, insecure output
handling, or model extraction.

If you're teaching OWASP LLM Top 10 concepts, running a workshop, or
just want to break something safely — it's free and self-hosted.
